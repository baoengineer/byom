Sign in, then run.

~/myapp
$ byoclaude login
$ byoclaude

Independent project, not affiliated with Anthropic or OpenAI. Claude requests are relayed on Claude Code’s own sign-in; turn that off with byoclaude config set relay false. MIT licensed, for macOS and Linux.
